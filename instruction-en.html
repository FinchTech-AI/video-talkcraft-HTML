Video TalkCraft Skill

From one continuous talking-head recording to captions, style comparisons, and targeted revisions.

This guide uses the supplied English talking-head recording to move from material inspection and transcript approval to a short preview, full output, style comparison, and a targeted revision. The full Skill also includes word-level timestamps, SHOTBOOK planning, motion templates, segmented Remotion rendering, audio/visual checks, and manual adjustment in a local workbench. The recording does not need preassigned shots, existing captions, or a separate audio file; first confirm that it contains audible speech.

You confirm the transcript, numerical claims, caption style, and final result. This case concentrates on two demonstrated capabilities: captioning a continuous recording, and changing caption style or size in the same segment.

Step 1 · Check the Skill and recording

Prompt

Install and check this Skill.

Expected response: the Skill’s runtime status, checked modules, and unverified areas. Before captioning, also confirm that the video has usable embedded speech. A missing sidecar audio file is not evidence that the recording has no sound.

Pass condition: the installation and basic checks are clear, and any missing production material is identified.

Step 2 · Prepare the transcript

Prompt

Write down what I say in the video, mark anything unclear, and let me confirm it. Keep the transcript in English.

Expected response: a transcript for this recording with uncertain words marked. The English run identified an uncertain phrase and asked for confirmation.

Pass condition: verify numbers, proper names, speech details, and omitted phrases. When a statement conflicts with the figures, check the source speech and facts first; do not silently rewrite the speaker’s point.

Step 3 · Make a short preview

Prompt

Pick a short section and add synced English captions and animations that emphasize key points. Use a clean, light style and keep my original voice and footage.

Expected response: a playable short preview and its captions. The English case first produced an opening excerpt of about 11 seconds. A continuous recording does not require you to name shots in advance.

Pass condition: readable text, a clear face, complete phrases at segment boundaries, and the requested voice and footage. Adjust the preview before continuing. An approved excerpt does not replace a review of the full recording.

Step 4 · Extend the approved style to the full recording

Prompt

The preview looks good. Apply this style to the whole recording without changing the content or original audio.

Send this after approving the preview. Expected response: a complete captioned recording. The English output is about 30.9 seconds, and its extracted AAC data matches the source.

Check line breaks beyond the opening

Pass condition: captions continue through the recording, content and sound remain complete, and the original is retained. Still frames help check layout; they do not replace a full viewing and listening pass.

Step 5 · Compare light and dark caption styles

Prompt

Restyle that same short section with a dark background. Keep the content, voice, and presenter unchanged, and show me both versions.

Expected response: light and dark versions of the same segment. Compare the same time point and inspect the caption card’s fill, text color, border, and legibility. A dark caption card is not a replacement of the presenter’s background.

Pass condition: both versions show the same content and corresponding presenter footage, with captions readable on a small screen. The initial English light preview has stereo audio while the other three comparison versions have mono audio; verify sound for each variant.

Step 6 · Locate a revision by the spoken phrase

Prompt

Make the captions larger when I say the price of Tesla, without covering my face. Leave everything else unchanged and keep the original version.

Name a phrase actually spoken in the recording. In this English case, the request targets the price statement; only “Three billion dollars.” is enlarged, and the original light and dark previews are retained.

Pass condition: the specified caption is larger and other captions are unchanged. Review its full on-screen interval, including line breaks, screen edges, face, and microphone. Save the original and revised versions separately.

Step 7 · Review and keep the editable deliverables

Prompt

Check the captions, visual overlaps, and audio, and tell me about any issues. Keep the finished video, editable project, comparison versions, and key screenshots.

Expected response: specific findings, the checked scope, unfinished items, and separately saved deliverables. Play the whole recording, then inspect numbers, caption entry/exit times, line breaks, and overlaps. Keep an actual review record if an independent reviewer is used.
